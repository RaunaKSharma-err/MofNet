# Grade 7 Social Studies — Nepal Curriculum

## Ancient History of Nepal

The Kathmandu Valley was home to ancient civilizations. Licchavi period (400-750 AD) was a golden age of art, architecture, and trade. Malla period (1200-1769 AD) saw development of cities, temples, and culture in Kathmandu, Bhaktapur, and Patan.

## Unification of Nepal

Prithvi Narayan Shah unified modern Nepal in the 18th century, starting from Gorkha. His policy of national unity emphasized preserving Nepal's independence between two large neighbors.

## Economic Activities

Major economic activities in Nepal include agriculture, tourism, hydropower, remittance, and small-scale industry. Agriculture employs the largest share of the population, especially in rural areas.

## Natural Resources

Nepal's natural resources include water (hydropower potential), forests, minerals, and scenic beauty for tourism. Sustainable use of resources is essential for long-term development.

## Human Rights and Democracy

Human rights are basic freedoms every person deserves: right to life, education, equality, and freedom of expression. Nepal's constitution guarantees fundamental rights. Democracy means government by the people through elected representatives.

## Disaster Preparedness

Nepal is prone to earthquakes, floods, and landslides. Preparedness includes emergency kits, evacuation plans, building safe structures, and community drills. Schools play an important role in disaster education.

## International Relations

Nepal maintains non-aligned foreign policy and friendly relations with neighboring countries and the international community. It is a member of the United Nations and SAARC.
