# Grade 7 Mathematics — Nepal Curriculum

## Integers

Integers include positive numbers, negative numbers, and zero: ..., -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ...

Rules for integer operations:
- Adding two negatives: add and keep the negative sign
- Subtracting an integer: add its opposite
- Multiplying/dividing: same signs give positive, different signs give negative

## Algebraic Expressions

A variable is a letter that represents an unknown number. An algebraic expression combines numbers, variables, and operations. Example: 3x + 5.

Like terms have the same variable raised to the same power. Simplify by combining like terms: 2x + 3x = 5x.

## Simple Equations

An equation states that two expressions are equal. To solve, perform the same operation on both sides to isolate the variable.

Example: x + 7 = 15 → x = 8.

## Percentage

Percentage means "per hundred." To convert a fraction to percentage, multiply by 100. 1/4 = 25%.

Simple interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) ÷ 100.

## Practical Geometry

Constructing angles and triangles using a compass and ruler. The sum of angles in a triangle is always 180°. The sum of angles around a point is 360°.

## Data Handling

Data can be organized in frequency tables and displayed using bar graphs and pie charts. Mean (average) = sum of all values ÷ number of values.
