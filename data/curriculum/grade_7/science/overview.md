# Grade 7 Science — Nepal Curriculum

## Cell Structure

The cell is the basic unit of life. Plant cells have a cell wall, cell membrane, nucleus, cytoplasm, chloroplasts, and vacuole. Animal cells have a cell membrane, nucleus, and cytoplasm but no cell wall or chloroplasts.

## Classification of Living Things

Living things are classified into five kingdoms: Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, and Animalia. Classification is based on characteristics like cell structure, mode of nutrition, and body organization.

## Heat and Temperature

Heat is a form of energy that flows from a hotter body to a colder body. Temperature measures the hotness or coldness of a body. The SI unit of temperature is Kelvin; Celsius is commonly used in daily life.

## Light and Reflection

Light travels in straight lines. When light hits a mirror, it reflects. The angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection. Mirrors can be plane, concave, or convex.

## Acids, Bases, and Salts

Acids taste sour and turn blue litmus red (e.g., lemon juice, vinegar). Bases taste bitter and turn red litmus blue (e.g., soap, baking soda). When an acid and base react, they form salt and water. This is called neutralization.

## Reproduction in Plants

Plants reproduce through sexual reproduction (seeds from flowers) and asexual reproduction (runners, tubers, grafting). Pollination is the transfer of pollen from anther to stigma. Fertilization leads to seed formation.

## Environmental Conservation

Deforestation, pollution, and climate change threaten Nepal's ecosystems. Conservation includes afforestation, wildlife protection, waste management, and using renewable energy sources like micro-hydro and solar power in remote areas.
