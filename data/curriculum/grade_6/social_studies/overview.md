# Grade 6 Social Studies — Nepal Curriculum

## Introduction to Social Studies

Social studies helps us understand our society, culture, history, geography, and civic life. In Nepal, social studies teaches students about their local community, nation, and relationship with the world.

## Nepal: Location and Physical Features

Nepal is a landlocked country in South Asia, located between India and China (Tibet). It lies roughly between 26°22' N and 30°27' N latitude and 80°04' E and 88°12' E longitude.

Nepal has three main geographical regions:
- Himalayan Region (north): High mountains including Mount Everest (8848.86 m)
- Hilly Region (middle): Hills, valleys, and river basins
- Terai Region (south): Flat fertile plains

## Climate and Vegetation

Climate varies with altitude. The Terai is hot in summer, the hills have moderate climate, and the Himalayas are cold with snow year-round. Nepal has diverse vegetation from tropical forests in the Terai to alpine meadows in the mountains.

## Local Government

Nepal is a federal democratic republic divided into provinces, districts, and local units (municipalities and rural municipalities). Local government provides services like education, health, roads, and sanitation to communities.

## Culture and Heritage

Nepal is culturally diverse with many ethnic groups, languages, festivals, and traditions. Important heritage sites include Pashupatinath, Swayambhunath, Lumbini (birthplace of Buddha), and Durbar Squares in the Kathmandu Valley.

## Civic Responsibility

Good citizens follow laws, pay taxes, participate in community activities, respect others, and protect public property and the environment.
