# Grade 6 Mathematics — Nepal Curriculum

## Natural Numbers and Whole Numbers

Natural numbers are counting numbers starting from 1: 1, 2, 3, 4, ... Whole numbers include all natural numbers plus zero: 0, 1, 2, 3, ...

## Fractions

A fraction represents a part of a whole. It has a numerator (top) and denominator (bottom). Equivalent fractions represent the same value, such as 1/2 = 2/4 = 3/6.

To add fractions with the same denominator, add the numerators and keep the denominator. To add fractions with different denominators, first find a common denominator.

## Decimals

Decimals are another way to represent fractions. The place values to the right of the decimal point are tenths, hundredths, and thousandths. For example, 0.5 = 5/10 = 1/2.

## Basic Geometry

A point has no size. A line extends endlessly in both directions. A line segment has two endpoints. An angle is formed when two rays meet at a common point called the vertex.

Types of angles: acute (less than 90°), right (exactly 90°), obtuse (between 90° and 180°), and straight (180°).

## Perimeter and Area

Perimeter is the total distance around a shape. Area is the space inside a shape.

Rectangle: Perimeter = 2 × (length + width). Area = length × width.
Triangle: Area = (base × height) ÷ 2.

## Ratio and Proportion

A ratio compares two quantities. If the ratio of boys to girls in a class is 3:2, it means for every 3 boys there are 2 girls. Proportion states that two ratios are equal.
