# Grade 8 Mathematics — Nepal Curriculum

## Rational Numbers

A rational number can be expressed as p/q where p and q are integers and q ≠ 0. Examples: 1/2, -3/4, 0.75, 5. Every integer is also a rational number.

## Exponents and Powers

Laws of exponents:
- aᵐ × aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ
- aᵐ ÷ aⁿ = aᵐ⁻ⁿ
- (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ
- a⁰ = 1 (a ≠ 0)

## Squares and Square Roots

A square of a number is the number multiplied by itself. The square root of a number is a value that, when multiplied by itself, gives the original number. √81 = 9.

## Algebraic Identities

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
(a - b)² = a² - 2ab + b²
(a + b)(a - b) = a² - b²

These identities simplify multiplication of binomials.

## Linear Equations in One Variable

Equations with one variable of degree one. Example: 2x - 5 = 11 → 2x = 16 → x = 8. Word problems translate real situations into equations.

## Quadrilaterals and Polygons

Properties of parallelogram, rectangle, rhombus, and square. Sum of interior angles of a quadrilateral is 360°. Sum of interior angles of a polygon with n sides = (n - 2) × 180°.

## Mensuration

Volume of cuboid = length × width × height.
Volume of cylinder = πr²h.
Surface area problems apply to real objects like water tanks and grain storage in rural schools.

## Introduction to Graphs

Coordinate plane with x-axis and y-axis. Plotting points (x, y). Linear graphs represent relationships like distance-time graphs.
