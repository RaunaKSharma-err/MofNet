# Grade 8 Science — Nepal Curriculum

## Force and Pressure

Force is a push or pull that can change the speed, direction, or shape of an object. Pressure is force per unit area: Pressure = Force ÷ Area. Unit of pressure is Pascal (Pa).

## Friction

Friction opposes motion between surfaces in contact. It is useful for walking and braking but also causes wear and energy loss. Friction can be reduced using lubricants, wheels, or smooth surfaces.

## Chemical Reactions

A chemical reaction produces new substances. Signs include color change, gas formation, temperature change, or precipitate formation. A balanced chemical equation shows equal numbers of atoms on both sides.

## Metals and Non-metals

Metals are lustrous, malleable, ductile, and good conductors of heat and electricity (e.g., iron, copper, aluminum). Non-metals are dull, brittle, and poor conductors (e.g., sulfur, carbon, oxygen).

## Electricity and Magnetism

Electric current is the flow of electric charge through a conductor. A circuit needs a source (battery), conductor (wire), and load (bulb). Electromagnets are made by wrapping wire around an iron core and passing current through it.

## Human Reproductive System

Adolescence is the transition from childhood to adulthood. The reproductive system enables human reproduction. Good nutrition, hygiene, and awareness are important during this stage of development.

## Ecosystem and Biodiversity

An ecosystem includes all living and non-living components interacting in an area. Nepal has rich biodiversity with many endemic species. Biodiversity conservation protects ecosystems for future generations.

## Public Health

Vaccination, clean water, mosquito control, and health education prevent diseases like typhoid, dengue, and tuberculosis. Community health workers serve vital roles in remote Himalayan villages.
