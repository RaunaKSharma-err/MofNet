# Grade 8 Social Studies — Nepal Curriculum

## Constitution of Nepal 2015

Nepal adopted a new constitution in 2015 (2072 BS). Key features include federal structure, secular state, fundamental rights, independent judiciary, and periodic elections. The constitution is the supreme law of the land.

## Federal Structure

Nepal has 7 provinces and 753 local levels. Power is divided among federal, provincial, and local governments. This decentralization aims to bring governance closer to citizens in remote areas.

## Economic Development

Nepal's development challenges include poverty, unemployment, infrastructure gaps, and dependence on remittance. Strategies include investment in education, agriculture modernization, tourism promotion, and hydropower development.

## Globalization

Globalization connects countries through trade, technology, and communication. It creates opportunities (access to markets, information) and challenges (cultural change, economic inequality). Nepal participates in global trade and international organizations.

## Media and Information Literacy

Media includes newspapers, radio, television, and the internet. Students should evaluate sources critically, identify misinformation, and use media responsibly. Radio remains an important medium in remote Himalayan communities with limited internet.

## Gender Equality and Social Inclusion

The constitution promotes gender equality and inclusion of marginalized groups. Equal access to education for girls and boys is essential for national development. Social inclusion ensures dalit, janajati, madhesi, and other communities participate fully in society.

## Sustainable Development Goals

Nepal is committed to the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) including quality education (SDG 4), clean water (SDG 6), and climate action (SDG 13). Local schools can contribute through environmental clubs and community projects.

## Citizenship and National Identity

Nepali citizenship connects individuals to the state with rights and duties. National identity celebrates diversity — multiple languages, religions, and cultures united under one nation while respecting differences.
